# Using IoT and Smart Sensors #
* IoT and Smart Sensors give real-time monitoring of waste levels and collection schedules
* notify waste removal services when bins are nearly complete, optimising collection routes and reducing unnecessary trips
* saving time and fuel

# AI and Machine learning 
* can promote innovative waste management by helping with decision-making tasks
* analyze info to find patterns and determine the best routes for waste collectin
* predict future waste production and improve recycling operations
