How to save fuel, time and money:

- the shortest route
-   speed limits, road closures, diversions, traffic lights and road conditions
- the order in which the stops are made
- optimal driving speed (55 to 65 mph but in a city this is not possible)
- stopping increases fuel consumption
- the lighter the vehicle the more efficient 
- maintaining a constant speed (i.e. not speeding up and slowing down)
